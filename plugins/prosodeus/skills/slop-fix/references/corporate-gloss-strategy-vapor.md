# Corporate gloss and strategy vapor

> Business-speak that polishes weak substance — verbs of agency, innovation adjectives, outcome nouns, and synergy markers substituting MBA-buzzword fluency for operational clarity.

**Hit list:** [`hitlists/corporate-gloss-strategy-vapor.json`](../hitlists/corporate-gloss-strategy-vapor.json)  
**Signal strength:** Moderate  
**False-positive risk:** Medium — business jargon has genuine uses  

## Why it reads as slop

Corporate gloss inflates ordinary capabilities into breakthroughs. *Strategy verbs* (*leverage, streamline, empower*) replace plain action words with vocabulary implying strategic sophistication. *Innovation adjectives* (*cutting-edge, seamless*) self-award technological status without validation. *Outcome nouns* (*value proposition, thought leadership*) abstract measurable results into unaccountable categories. *Capability claims* (*best-in-class, world-class*) assert supremacy without comparison methodology.

## What it looks like

*Leverage* — universal substitute for “use.” *Unlock potential* — quintessential AI-slop implying hidden value only the vendor can access. *Best-in-class* — self-awarded superlative with no class definition. *Actionable insights* — pleonasm; insights that are not actionable are not insights. *Moving forward* — temporal throat-clearing.

## How to rewrite it

Apply these strategies from [strategies.md](strategies.md), in this order of preference:

- **Specify**: replace abstraction with concrete nouns, dates, people and mechanisms: who did what, when, with what result.
- **De-hype**: replace the amplifier with the measured effect.
- **Add authorship**: add lived observation, domain knowledge and a point of view only the writer has.

Use the `replacement` field in the hit list as a starting point, not a drop-in. A replacement that does not carry a real fact is just a different filler phrase.

## When not to flag it

A single hit is a prompt to check the sentence, not a verdict. Flag a passage when markers cluster (two or more per 100 words is worth a look, five or more is a strong signal) and when the text could have said something more specific. Never treat a hit as evidence of who or what wrote the text. See [the editorial policy](../docs/editorial-policy.md).
